### 4. Posterior variance

[Posterior variance](https://www.johndcook.com/blog/2026/07/12/posterior-variance/) — **johndcook.com** · 07-12 19:26 · ⭐ 15/30

> The article examines whether adding more data always reduces posterior variance in Bayesian statistics, answering that it does not. The posterior mean is a weighted average of the prior mean and the data mean, and if new data conflicts strongly with the prior, the posterior variance can increase. This is demonstrated through Bayesian models where the prior and data have different means. The key takeaway is that the effect of additional data on variance depends on the model and the consistency of the data with the prior.
